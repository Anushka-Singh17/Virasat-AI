## Licence note

CC BY-SA means you may use and adapt the photos, **if** you give credit (author, licence, link) and share
adaptations under the same licence. The resized copies in this folder are adaptations, so they stay under the
same CC BY-SA licence as their originals. The rest of the project's code is not affected by this licence.
